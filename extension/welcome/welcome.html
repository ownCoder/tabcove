Your tabs are safe here.

Tabcove saves your open tabs in one click, keeps them searchable, and makes sure you can always get them back.

Counting your open tabs…

  • Nothing is ever final

    Every destructive action saves a restore point first. Deleted collections wait 30 days in an undo bin. There is always a way back.

  • Find any tab in a second

    Search across every saved title, address, and collection name — instantly, even with tens of thousands of tabs saved.

  • Nothing leaves this device

    No account, no sign-in, no analytics. Tabcove contains no networking code at all and asks for no access to any website.

How stowing works

  1. 1
    Your tabs are saved first. Nothing closes until the save has completed, so a failure can never cost you a tab.
  2. 2
    Then the tabs close and your browser gets its memory back. Pinned tabs stay open by default.
  3. 3
    Your library opens on what you just saved — with tab groups, names, and colours intact.

Prefer to keep the tabs open? Turn off “Close tabs after stowing” in settings.

Coming from another tab manager?

Tabcove imports OneTab's exported text, its own JSON backups, and any plain list of web addresses. A restore point is saved before anything is written.

Shortcuts: AltShiftS to stow · AltShiftL for your library
