Hosting
-------

The policy is published with GitHub Pages from the `gh-pages` branch of
https://github.com/ownCoder/tabcove — so every revision is public and
timestamped, which is exactly what a reviewer wants to be able to check.

The page source lives at `site/privacy.html` on the `main` branch. To publish an
update:

    1. Edit site/privacy.html and change the "Last updated" date.
    2. Commit to main.
    3. Copy the contents of site/ onto the gh-pages branch and push.

Do not change the URL. It is recorded in the store listing, in the extension's
options page, and in the manifest's homepage_url.
